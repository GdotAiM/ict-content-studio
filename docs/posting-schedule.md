# Posting schedule (suggestions only — Ntloso posts by hand)
All times SAST (Africa/Johannesburg, UTC+2, no DST). NY times shift by 1 hour when the US changes clocks;
convert with `python3 scripts/et_to_sast.py 09:30 --date YYYY-MM-DD`.

| Slot | SAST (US EDT, ~Mar–Nov) | SAST (US EST, ~Nov–Mar) | Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pre-open concept | ~13:00–14:00 | ~14:00–15:00 | Term of the day (`templates/ict-term-of-the-day.md`), cheat sheet, curated ICT source. Concepts only, no levels for today. |
| Session review | ~19:00–20:00 | ~20:00–21:00 | Past-tense review of the 09:30–11:00 ET session (thread / recap), or `no-trade-day-writer` post. |
| Weekly journal | Saturday, any time | Saturday | `paper-journal-writer` "Paper Journal Wk N" thread, losses included. |
| Research refresh | First weekend of the month | | `x-research-scout` (read-only). |

Why: the pre-open slot lands before the NY open (09:30 ET = 15:30 SAST in EDT / 16:30 in EST); the review lands after the NY AM session ends (11:00 ET = 17:00 / 18:00 SAST).
